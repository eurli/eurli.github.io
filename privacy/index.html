Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This policy explains how TechTed Publisher (the "app"), the internal tool that publishes videos to the TechTed YouTube channel, handles information. The app is operated by the owner of the TechTed channel ("we", "us") and is used only by that owner, for that one channel.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app may access:

  • Upload and manage videos on the authorised channel (YouTube Data API): to upload videos, set their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy status, add thumbnails and upload caption files;
  • Channel analytics reports (YouTube Analytics API, read-only): aggregated statistics for the authorised channel, such as views, watch time, audience retention and traffic sources;
  • basic channel information needed to identify the authorised channel (channel ID and name).

The app does not request access to Gmail, contacts, Google Drive or any other Google service, and it does not access other YouTube channels.

How the information is used

  • to publish TechTed videos and their metadata to the channel;
  • to read the channel's own performance reports so we can decide which topics and formats to make next.

Analytics are aggregated reports provided by YouTube. The app does not collect, profile or track individual viewers, and it does not use the data for advertising.

Storage and security

  • The Google authorisation (OAuth refresh token) is stored as an encrypted secret in the private source code repository that runs the app, and is only used by the app's automated jobs.
  • Analytics reports may be saved as summary files in the same private repository so that trends can be compared over time.
  • We do not store passwords. Sign-in happens on Google's own pages.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share data obtained through YouTube API Services with anyone. The only service providers involved are those that run the app's private automation (GitHub) and, for writing scripts, AI model providers that receive public news text only, never YouTube account data or analytics.

Retention and deletion

  • Saved analytics summaries are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted.
  • You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings (also available at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). When access is revoked, the stored authorisation stops working and is deleted from the repository's secrets within 7 days, and stored analytics data is deleted within 30 days.
  • To ask for deletion of any stored data, use the contact link below.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies or tracking, and the app does not place cookies on viewers' devices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or data requests: open a request on this site's GitHub page. Please don't include personal details in a public request; we will reply there and arrange a private channel if needed.
