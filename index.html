AI tools & automation, explained

TechTed is a YouTube channel that breaks down new AI models, apps and automation tools in plain English: what was announced, what it means for you, and what to watch out for.

Made with AI, checked against sources. Ted is an AI-generated presenter with an AI voice. Each script is written with AI from published announcements and news reports, fact-checked against those sources, and every video lists its sources in the description. Videos are labelled as altered or synthetic content on YouTube.

TechTed Publisher

TechTed Publisher is the small internal tool that runs the channel. It is used only by the channel owner, for the TechTed YouTube channel, and it uses YouTube API Services to:

  • upload finished videos, thumbnails and captions to the TechTed channel;
  • read the channel's own YouTube Analytics (views, watch time, audience retention) to decide which topics to cover next.

It does not access any other channel, does not collect data about viewers beyond the aggregated reports YouTube Analytics provides to the channel owner, and does not sell or share data. Details are in the Privacy Policy. Use of the tool is subject to the Terms of Service, the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
